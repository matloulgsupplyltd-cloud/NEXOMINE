Corporate governance

Accountable leadership

Strong governance underpins trust. Publish the structures and policies that guide the company.

Board of directors

Our board combines executive and independent non-executive directors, led by an independent chairperson. It sets strategy, oversees risk and holds management to account.

Board committees

Audit and risk, remuneration and nominations, and social, ethics and sustainability committees each operate under written terms of reference.

Codes and policies

Our code of conduct, anti-bribery and corruption policy, human rights policy and supplier code apply to all employees and contractors.

Ethics and whistleblowing

Concerns can be raised confidentially through an independent ethics line. Reports are reviewed by the audit and risk committee.
