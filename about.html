Who we are

A responsible mining company with an eye on the long term

Lundana Mining Company is an Angolan diamond producer, founded in 2009 and headquartered in Luanda. We employ around 2,400 people and contractors across two mining operations and a treatment plant in Lunda Norte province.

Our rough diamonds are sold through authorised Angolan channels to buyers who expect certified, traceable supply. We earn that trust by running safe, efficient mines and by treating our people, our neighbours and the land with respect.

Our history

How we got here

From one open-cast pit to a three-site producer, our growth has been steady and deliberate.

  1. 2009

    Lundana Mining Company is founded in Luanda by a team of Angolan mining engineers and geologists, with a prospecting licence in Lunda Norte.

  2. 2012

    First kimberlite ore is produced at Mukanda Ridge Mine.

  3. 2016

    The Cacuilo Valley Alluvial Operation begins recovering diamonds from river gravels.

  4. 2019

    The Dundo Treatment Plant is commissioned, bringing dense-media separation and sorting under one roof.

  5. 2022

    The Lundana Community Foundation is established to channel funding into education, water and local enterprise.

  6. 2026

    Construction starts on the Sol Nascente Solar Project, our first on-site renewable energy plant.
