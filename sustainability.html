Sustainability

Responsible resource management, climate action and community benefit.

EnvironmentWater and biodiversityClimate and energyCommunitiesHealth and safety
Environmental protection

Protecting land from first plan to final closure

We design every site with its closure in mind. That means managing dust, noise and waste carefully, and rehabilitating land progressively instead of waiting until the end.

  • Environmental impact assessment before development
  • Progressive rehabilitation behind active mining
  • Waste, tailings and dust management systems

Responsible resource management

We aim to recover more of each orebody with less waste, using better planning, sorting and processing, and by reusing materials where we can.

Water conservation

Using less water, and returning it clean

Water is shared with communities and ecosystems. We measure, recycle and treat water at every site and report on our performance.

  • Closed-loop recycling in processing plants
  • Monitoring of surface and ground water
  • Treatment before any discharge

Biodiversity

We map habitats before we disturb land, avoid sensitive areas where possible and rehabilitate with locally appropriate species.

Climate and energy

Cutting carbon and moving to renewable power

Energy and fuel are our largest sources of emissions. We are working to use less, switch to cleaner sources and measure progress transparently.

  • Renewable energy projects at and near our sites
  • Fleet and plant efficiency programmes
  • Greenhouse gas measurement and reporting

The Sol Nascente Solar Project, now under construction at Mukanda Ridge, is the first step in our roadmap to bring renewable power to all our operations.

Community development

Mining should leave communities stronger

We work with our neighbours to agree priorities, create local opportunity and share benefit.

Local employment

Training and hiring from host communities wherever we operate.

Local business

Procurement that gives local suppliers a fair chance to compete.

Education and skills

Bursaries, learnerships and skills programmes for young people.

Health and safety

Everyone goes home safe, every day

Safety is our first value. Every employee and contractor has the authority and the duty to stop work they believe is unsafe.

  • Critical-control management for the highest-risk activities
  • Visible felt leadership from site managers
  • Occupational health and wellness programmes
  • Open reporting and shared learning from incidents
