Who we are

A mining company built on discipline, care and long-term thinking.

Founded in 2009 and headquartered in Luanda, Lundana Mining Company produces gem-quality and industrial diamonds from the Lunda Norte province, in the north-east of Angola.

We run two mining operations and a treatment plant in Lunda Norte. Our approach joins proven operating practice with new technology, so that every tonne is mined safely, efficiently and with a clear plan for the land afterwards.

About our company
What we do

From orebody to market, run with care

Four capabilities sit behind everything we deliver.

Mining

Open-cast and alluvial methods planned around safety, recovery and rehabilitation.

Processing

Dense-media separation, sorting and quality control that turn run-of-mine material into saleable rough diamonds.

Technology

Fleet, plant and geological data used to cut waste and make better decisions earlier.

Stewardship

Water, energy, land and biodiversity managed as part of the plan, not an afterthought.
