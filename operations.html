Mining operations

Open-cast and alluvial mining, planned for safety and recovery

Open-cast mining

Open-cast operations use drilling, blasting, loading and hauling in carefully designed benches. Mine plans balance recovery, stability, water management and the final landform.

  • Bench design set by geotechnical data
  • Fleet dispatch to cut idle time and fuel use
  • Progressive rehabilitation behind the working face

Alluvial mining

Alluvial operations recover diamonds from river gravels using excavators, wet screening and dense-media separation. Water, riverbank and land controls are designed in from the first plan.

  • Settling ponds and closed-loop water recycling
  • Riverbank buffers and erosion control
  • Strict access control and secure handling of recovered stones

Mineral resources

What we produce

Kimberlite diamonds

Gem-quality and industrial rough diamonds recovered at Mukanda Ridge and sorted at the Dundo plant.

Alluvial diamonds

Rough stones recovered from river gravels in the Cacuilo valley, prized for their quality and character.

Sorted and certified parcels

Parcels sorted, valued and documented at Dundo, with traceability from mine to sale.

Formal resource and reserve statements are published in our annual reporting, available in the Investors section.

Processing facilities

From run-of-mine ore to certified rough diamonds

Our treatment plant crushes, screens and concentrates ore, then sorts recovered stones under strict security. Plant control systems track throughput and quality continuously, so problems are caught early.

  • Crushing and screening circuits
  • Dense-media separation
  • Sorting, valuation and certification
  • Secure storage and dispatch
